# First driving playtest — October 14, 2026

Open `http://127.0.0.1:5173/drive.html` with the dev server running. The seed atlas remains at `/`. This loop is authored and independent of the seed, so the handling can be tuned before procedural road integration.

## Try this in five minutes

1. Start driving. Hold W or ↑ for a few seconds. Release it, then brake with S or ↓. Braking should stop without engaging reverse.
2. Follow the loop with A/D or ←/→, starting around 35–50 km/h. Try a full lap. Notice the bump on the first straight, the rise around the far turn and the lower-grip gravel on the return straight.
3. Try a short Space handbrake tap in a corner. Let go and recover the line. Scrape a guardrail at a modest speed; the car should stay inside it. Press R if stuck.
4. Stop, press V, then W to reverse. Stop and press V again for forward. The HUD shows the gear actually engaged; a message explains when reverse is pending.
5. Pause with P or Escape. Switch tabs while holding throttle, return and resume; the old key must not remain held. Restart the session from Telemetry.
6. If available, connect a standard controller: left stick steering, RT throttle, LT brake, A handbrake, X recovery, Y reverse, Menu pause. Return controls to neutral after resuming. Check drift and fine steering. On a narrow viewport, use the touch buttons.

Useful feedback: steering too slow/too sharp, braking distance, whether the car feels planted or slippery, camera comfort, and the location/speed of any unexpected bounce or collision. Record browser, device, input method and whether this was the first lap.

## Evidence collected by implementation

- Native scripted driver completed ten consecutive laps at approximately 50 km/h, crossing the hill, bump and gravel, with zero impacts/recoveries. Minimum upright dot .9972; no airborne ticks. This is stability evidence, not a human first-lap test.
- Native tests cover spring equilibrium, acceleration, stopping, explicit reverse, driver-right steering, guardrail boundary, rollover recovery, malformed inputs, road sample/collision agreement and repeat replay.
- Native/WASM 60-second replay agrees within .000008 m at sampled positions on this Mac/toolchain. Seed golden manifests remain unchanged.
- Browser production tests cover startup, acceleration/braking, pause/recovery/restart, input clearing, nested paths, touch controls, simulated standard controller and actionable missing-WASM failures.
- Manual in-app browser inspection caught and corrected a coordinate-system steering sign error and an overlay/HUD overlap. Desktop and narrow viewports inspected; acceleration and corrected steering observed.
