## Chapter 4 — Venus: "Proof"

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Verb | **Surrender** |
| Site | Cythera Port (fictional): an aerostat town floating at 52 km in the cloud deck, where pressure is about two-thirds of Earth's and the temperature is near 37 °C (real conditions at that height) |
| Sky | Above: bright cream haze, the Sun a soft disc 0.74° wide. Below: a floor of yellow-white cloud to every horizon, moving. The port circles the planet with the wind once every four days, so a day here is 48 hours of light: start at mid-morning. |
| Arrival | No ground. The descent ends with *Wren* caught on a docking trapeze under the port's keel. |
| Vehicle | Gantry pod on the keel rail (Rail, 4 m/s) |
| People | Priya Nair, Capt. Ines Varga, Ensign Cal Osei |
| Length | 30–40 min. The shortest chapter. Do not pad it. |
| Budget | 1 port exterior (keel, three halls, envelope overhead), 1 cutter, 1 press, 45 lines |

### Stations along the keel
`ve_trapeze` (arrivals) → `ve_gallery` (observation; open to the cloud deck: postcard 5) → `ve_proof` (proofing hall) → `ve_patrol` (Patrol dock, empty on arrival).

### Proofing
The six cores ride a slow overhead conveyor from the trapeze to the hall. At the press, for each core:
1. `Steer` turns the core on its cradle until its witness mark meets the press's (±5°).
2. `Interact`: the press comes down. A deep, clean thud. Priya lifts the plate; the proof mark is there.

Priya speaks while she works: where she is from, what the mark means, that she has done four thousand and never had one fail. After the fifth: "People think a stamp is a small thing. It's a stranger saying *I checked.*" As the sixth thud lands, a long shadow slides across the hall windows. The *Harrier* is docking.

### The requisition (H3)
The pod is called back to the trapeze. The six proofed cores are back on *Wren*'s rack. Captain Varga stands on the gantry with Ensign Osei. She reads Requisition Order 7 in four sentences. The view moves to the rack's latch panel: six physical latches, each a red handle under a guard.

- Each latch: hold `Interact` 2.5 s. The handle turns through 90°; there is no on-screen meter, only the handle. Releasing early lets it spring back.
- After each, a Patrol hoist lifts that core away past the camera, and one cradle on the hold monitor goes dark.
- **Nothing else can be done.** The pod will not move. `Recover`, the map and the cabin are unavailable. Photo mode and Look Home work.
- **The game waits forever.** At 60 s without input, Varga: "Take the time you need." At 180 s: "I can't leave without them, Courier. Neither of us wants me to do this part." Nothing further. She never takes them herself.
- After the third latch, Osei, quietly: "Sorry." Varga: "Ensign."
- After the sixth: Varga holds out the receipt. `Interact` takes it. "It's a good signature. It will be honoured." ★ Then, turning away: "I hope you never find out what it was for."

No music from the moment the shadow appears. After the *Harrier* undocks: wind, the envelope creaking, and the conveyor still running, empty, until Priya switches it off.

### After
Priya comes to the trapeze alone and gives the blank certificate (thread 10). The receipt becomes the keepsake. The journal's intention changes to: "Appeal at the Meridian Registry, Earth orbit." Launch and docking are normal; the rack is empty and in view.

### Stages
0 arrive → 1 gallery passed → 2–7 six stamps → 8 recalled → 9–14 six latches → 15 receipt → 16 Priya → 17 launch.

### Acceptance
- No tester finds a way to keep the cores, and none reports looking for a combat or escape option for more than a minute.
- In interview, a majority describe Varga as "doing her job" or similar, not as an enemy.
- The latch inputs and animation rig are a shared asset used again, mirrored, at Triton.

## Chapter 5 — Earth: "Position forty-one"

| | |

| Verb | **Read** |
| Site | The Meridian Registry (fictional): a ring station in geostationary orbit, 35,786 km up |
| Sky | **Earth, 17° wide, below and beside every window for the whole chapter.** Real continents, real cloud, turning once per 24 real minutes. The Moon passes once. The Courier never goes down. |
| Arrival | The *Kestrel* docks to the ring itself with the simple depot procedure. No lander, no descent. |
| Vehicle | Tether car on the ring's inner rail (Rail, 9 m/s, 500 m circuit) |
| People | Tomás Ibarra, Agnes Lindqvist |
| Length | 45–55 min |
| Budget | 1 ring exterior, 5 station close views, 12 documents, 70 lines |

### Stations
`ea_dock` → `ea_gallery` (window gallery: postcard 6) → `ea_appeals` → `ea_freight` (thread 3) → `ea_stacks` → `ea_yard` (chart room).

A station is a seated close view, like a cabin focus point.

### The appeal (stage 1)
Ninety seconds. Agnes Lindqvist reads the receipt, asks one question, hears one reply from three (tone only), and denies the appeal under Article 9. "You're not wrong to ask. I'm not allowed to be moved by it." Tomás, at the back with a stamp pad, says he will see the denial filed properly.

### The stacks (stages 2–6)
A desk under a lamp. Tomás opens a cabinet, says "I'll be about twenty minutes finding the right stamp," and leaves.

**Desk mechanic.** Up to four documents lie side by side. Each has several selectable **fields** (underlined on focus). Select a field on one document, then a field on another: if they are the same fact, a pin and a length of string join them and stay. If not, the string falls. Drawers hold twelve documents; any can be swapped onto the desk. Nothing can be failed or lost.

| Link | From | To | What it tells |
| ---: | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Receipt: *Order 7* | Requisition register: *Order 7* | Carrier: freighter *Marigold*, voyage 88. Consignee: code **TR-01**. Destination: sealed. |
| 2 | Register: *Marigold v.88* | Carrier log: *Marigold v.88* | Venus → Titan for propellant → (sealed). **Last report: Titan approach, nineteen days ago.** |
| 3 | Register: *TR-01* | Settlement index: *TR-01* | **Vigil Station, Triton. Population 212.** |
| 4 | Index: *Vigil Station* | Emergency docket: *Vigil Station* | Core set forty-four years old. Output 34%. Replacement requested six years ago. |

Distractors include thirty years of Hearth's own requests, every one initialled *T.I.*, and the queue ledger with Hearth at **41**. They link to nothing and are there to be read.

When the fourth string is pinned, Tomás is in the doorway. "Ah. You've found my misfiling."

### After the stacks
| Stage | Where | What |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| 7 | Stacks | **`choice_complaint`.** Tomás puts a complaint form on the desk, already filled in, naming Captain Varga. Sign (hold `Interact` 1.5 s on the signature line) or slide it back (`Back`). He comments on neither. |
| 8 | Stacks | Thread 11: Tomás asks, awkwardly, whether he might hold something from Mars. The shelf's keepsakes appear on the desk; the player may give one, or decline. ★ If given: he holds it in both hands. "Thirty years. I've written 'Hearth Crater' eleven hundred times. It's heavier than I thought." |
| 9 | Yard | The outer chart is handed over. A stamp impression on a card: the keepsake. |
| 10 | Dock | Mara's sixth letter is waiting (full text, file 11). |

### H4 — the plot
After stage 10 the journal's intention line is blank. No marker exists. The map opens normally. **Titan must be selected by the player.**

- Selecting Titan: the route draws; the intention becomes "Find the *Marigold*."
- Selecting Mars instead is allowed. On arrival Mara says by radio: "You're early. You did read it?" Hearth is unchanged; nothing is lost; Titan still waits.
- After ten real minutes with neither, one letter from Tomás: "The *Marigold*'s last position is on your chart, should it be useful. — T.I."

### Acceptance
- 85% of testers complete the four links in under twelve minutes with no hint.
- 90% plot Titan within three minutes of reading Mara's letter, and in interview say it was their decision.
- Look Home from the gallery puts Mars and Earth in one frame on the reference day.

### Sonar
In flight, `Interact` sends a ping (one per 3 s). Metal returns an echo: a tone whose delay is the range (1 s per 500 m) and whose stereo position is the bearing; a 2 s ring on the HUD repeats both for players without sound. Range 1,200 m. Four contacts exist: a mooring block, a drowned survey drone, the buoy, the pod. Over a contact, hovering below 8 m, hold `Interact` 3 s to drop a line and identify it.

### Fog beacons (thread 12)
A dark beacon is relit by hovering at its lamp and holding `Interact` 3 s (a breaker, as at a depot). Lit beacons glow to 900 m. **On the flight home visibility falls to 90 m.** A player who relit the three has an unbroken chain of lights back; one who did not must use sonar on the harbour's own marker. Either works.

| 7 | Quay. Talk to Varga. | See below. The Verona code. |

| 9 | Apron | Launch. Docking begins in Titan's shadow, ring lights only. |

### Varga on the quay (stage 7)
If `choice_complaint` was signed, she begins: "I've read it. It's accurate." If not: "You came. I wouldn't have."

Then, in both cases: she has run the dredge for nineteen days to a depth it cannot reach. She tells the Courier what the cores were for, not knowing they already know. The player's reply (one of three) may tell her. She is quiet. Then: the Compact kept a billet store at the Verona Works, Miranda; mothballed forty years; she is forbidden to disclose it and forbidden to leave her grounded ship. She writes the access code on the back of a requisition form. ★ "I took six from you with a piece of paper. Here's another piece of paper."

### Acceptance
- No tester is lost for more than three minutes: sonar on the harbour marker always works.
- The echo profile at stage 5 is understood without words by 90% of testers.
- A player who relit the beacons remarks, unprompted, on the flight back at least 40% of the time.

## Chapter 8 — Triton: "Vigil"

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Verb | **Give** |
| Site | Vigil Station (fictional), on the edge of Triton's southern cap (real terrain), 30 AU from the Sun |
| Sky | Black. **The Sun is the brightest star: a point, with 1/900 of Earth's light — a deep twilight, about three hundred full moons.** Neptune, 8° wide, deep blue, a third of the way up the northern sky. On the horizon, two real nitrogen geysers: thin dark plumes 8 km high, bent by a wind that should not exist. |
| Ground | Pink-white nitrogen frost, a rumpled plain. Real gravity 0.78 m/s². |
| Vehicle | Sledge with a six-core rack |
| Playable | 1,100 m of plain from apron to station; the station's ramp and reactor hall (driven into) |
| People | Annika Sørensen, Davit Kalandadze, Mika; 209 others as lamps, windows and voices; later Varga |
| Length | 50–60 min |
| Budget | 1 station exterior (mostly buried: mast, dome, ramp, 30 windows), 1 reactor hall interior, 212 hand lamps (instanced), 90 lines |

### Arrival
The descent's reveal shows a station with nine windows lit, amber. Annika is on the apron with a hand lamp. She does not ask what is on the rack. "We heard your drive two days ago. We didn't like to hope." If the Expedition Day is past 200: "We'd started closing the far corridors. You can open them again."

Pip's ninth question arrives here. Postcard 9 can be taken at any time.

### The drive in
1,100 m at 8 m/s. Halfway, the station's long east wing is passed: dark, frost on the inside of the windows. Davit talks about the old set: which core he calls the stubborn one. The ramp goes down into the hill.

### The reactor hall (H5)
A round hall, the old reactor a low drum in the floor with six sockets around it, each holding a dull, dark core. A wall board of six lamps, each labelled: NURSERY · GROW HALL · OBSERVATORY · LONG CORRIDOR · COMMONS · HOMES. Two are lit.

As the Sledge parks, the Courier's question is sent to Hearth automatically: the mail glyph shows *"Sent. 4 h 22 min each way."*

For each of six sockets, in any order:
1. Davit's hoist lifts the old core out (authored, 4 s).
2. The view is the rack's latch panel: the same six red handles as Venus. Hold `Interact` 2.5 s. The handle turns the other way. The new core is swung across and lowered into the socket.
3. The socket seals. One lamp on the board comes up. Over the open station channel, that section answers:

| # | Section | What is heard |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| 1 | Nursery | Several children at once, then one adult laughing and failing to hush them |
| 2 | Grow hall | A single voice: "Oh." Then: "Oh, they're all coming on." |
| 3 | Observatory | "We have the dome. We have the dome drive. Davit, we have the *dome*." |
| 4 | Long corridor | Footsteps, running, getting fainter |
| 5 | Commons | Many voices; someone starts to sing and is joined |
| 6 | Homes | Nothing for four seconds. Then doors. |

Davit, after the sixth: ★ "Forty-four years the old ones did. I'd like to keep one. Not for anything."

The game waits forever before each hold, as at Venus. Nobody prompts.

### Coming out
The ramp door opens on a different station: every window lit, the dome turning, and along both sides of the track to the apron, two hundred people in suits, each holding a lamp. Nobody speaks on the radio. The Sledge is driven between them. This is 1,100 m and should take a little over two minutes and must not be shortened.

Mara's answer arrives about nine real minutes after the first core, wherever the player is (held until any hinge input ends). Full text in file 11.

### Afterwards (any order)
| What | Where | Detail |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Threads 5 and 13 | Annika, commons airlock | The ice core; the *Marigold*'s log. She reads the log's last page aloud. |
| Thread 15 | Commons window | Every window has paper stars taped inside. Mika, behind the glass, holds up a drawing, then sends it out through the sample lock. Paper star keepsake. |
| Thread 9 | Reactor hall | Any photograph with the lit hall qualifies; send to Yusuf. |
| **`choice_testimony`** | Commons airlock, after the *Harrier* lands (two fiction days: a 15 s time-lapse of Neptune's phase, offered when the player chooses "Wait for the *Harrier*") | Annika: the council is writing down what happened, for the Registry. "You were there for both halves. What should it say about the Captain?" Three replies. Varga is on the channel and hears it. She does not respond to the answer, whichever it is. |
| Drop tanks | Apron | Vigil's tender fits them. Annika: "It's every spare tonne. Don't argue. We've got a reactor." |

### Stages
0 land → 1 Annika → 2 hall reached → 3–8 six cores → 9 driven out → 10 testimony given (required to launch; "say nothing" is a choice) → 11 launch. The docking that follows has no music and no variation; the rack is empty.

### Acceptance
- H5 and H3 use the same input, duration and rig; testers notice the echo (≥ 60% mention Venus when asked "did this remind you of anything?").
- No tester asks whether they could have kept the cores. If more than one in ten does, the scene has failed and the build-up must be reworked, not the choice added.
- The drive out is never interrupted by a notification.

## Chapter 9 — The long way home

One leg: Neptune → Mars, 31.6 AU, drive tier III, drop tanks, **10 min 31 s of cruise with no required stop**. The player may stop anywhere; nothing asks them to.

Beats are placed by fraction of the leg. All are passive unless the player chooses to engage.

| 0.33 | 3:28 | Albatross Light flashes. Its note: "Lit for the Kestrel. Safe home." |

| 0.67 | 7:03 | Past Saturn's orbit. The window's patch of sunlight is visible on the cabin wall again. The bean has turned back toward it. |

### Acceptance

- At least 60% of testers stay for the whole leg without using a phone or leaving the seat (observed).
- Nobody reports the leg as too long in more than one in five interviews. If they do, tune drive tier III up to 3,600 su/s before cutting any beat.

## After the credits

The game continues from Home 24.

- **Free travel.** Every site can be revisited. Each has a changed state and each named person has two new lines.
- **Sleep through the Quiet.** In the cabin, a bunk focus point appears: it skips the current cruise leg to its drop-out point. Available only after the credits.
- **Open threads** can still be completed (file 04 §5.5). Dark windows light one per round trip.
- **Vigil** on a return visit is fully lit, has re-opened its east wing, and has put the old "stubborn" core on a plinth in the commons.
- **Miranda** on a return visit: the works' lights are still on. Nobody turned them off. Nobody will.
- **The milestone buoy** keeps every mark.
- There is no New Game Plus and no second ending.
